Steel takeoffs are the foundation of every profitable bid. They are also where the most expensive mistakes get made. A single missed connection plate, a misclassified beam, or a missed drawing revision can trigger material shortages, schedule disruptions, and margin loss that no amount of pricing skill downstream can recover.
